HOW TO PLAY

1. Hold your phone upright, screen facing you.

2. Walk in place or take real steps. Motion bumps move the little explorer forward.

3. Turn your body with the phone to steer through the corridors.

4. Reach the red flag to escape the maze.

Motion needs HTTPS and your browser's sensor permission. Stay aware of your surroundings; walking in place is safest.

CONTROL MODE

Manual: hold arrows or use W / A / S / D. The compass follows your avatar, not the phone screen.
